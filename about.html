About Mood Meter

220+ Vibes. Zero Noise.

Mood Meter was created by Srikanth Nandiraju with a simple belief: checking in with your emotions should take two seconds, never require an account, never show an ad, and never upload your private journal to someone else's cloud.

Every tile on this canvas represents one of the expressive moods inside the app—from Joyful and In Love to Thinking, Tired, and Relieved. Inside Mood Meter on iOS and Android, you can search 220+ moods, create unlimited Custom Vibes, rate 1–5 intensity, and inspect on-device trends—100% free forever.
