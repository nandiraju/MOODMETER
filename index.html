Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mood Meter really 100% free?

Yes. Mood Meter is completely free with no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no locked "Pro" paywalls. Every feature—including all 220+ moods, custom vibes, and full analytics—is unlocked for everyone.

Where is my mood and journal data stored?

All mood entries, custom vibes, intensity ratings, and journal reflections are stored exclusively on your device. Mood Meter requires no account, maintains no cloud database of your entries, and includes zero third-party trackers. See our Privacy Policy.

How many moods can I track, and can I create custom moods?

Mood Meter comes with over 220 expressive built-in mood icons that you can browse or filter via instant search. You can also open Custom Vibes to create, name, edit, or delete your own personal emotions paired with any icon.

What analytics and insights does Mood Meter provide?

The Analytics tab calculates your Current Vibe Streak, Vibe Power Gauge, per-mood contribution donut gauges, a 7-day Intensity Trend chart, top-5 Vibe Distribution, Hourly Activity bars, and 8 key metrics including Days Tracked, Average Intensity, Common Mood, 7-Day Average change, Best/Worst Day, High Intensity %, and Mood Diversity.

Can I log past moods or edit my journal history?

Yes. When capturing a vibe, you can pick any date and time to backdate an entry, use keyboard voice dictation for quick reflections, search your entire History by mood name or note text, and tap any past entry to edit or delete it.

Which devices does Mood Meter support?

Mood Meter is available for iPhone and iPad on the Apple App Store and for Android phones and tablets on Google Play, featuring adaptive multi-column layouts and built-in Light and Dark themes.
