Detailed Breakdown

Who Each App Suits Best

Every mood tracker makes different trade-offs between simplicity, cloud sync, clinical depth, and pricing. Here is where each one shines—and where it falls short.

01 Our App
100% Free Forever

Mood Meter

Built around zero-friction check-ins: pick from 220+ expressive mood icons or create unlimited Custom Vibes, slide a 1–5 intensity scale, add optional voice-dictated reflections, and explore on-device streaks, 7-day Intensity Trends, and Hourly Activity charts.

Who it suits: People who want a completely free, ad-free, no-account mood journal where every chart and custom mood stays unlocked and data never leaves the phone.

Honest trade-off: Because Mood Meter has zero cloud servers, there is no cross-device sync or automatic cloud recovery if you lose your phone without an OS backup.

02 Micro-Diary & Habits
Freemium

Daylio

Daylio pioneered the tap-to-log micro-diary: choose one of your moods, tap activities you did (exercise, reading, sleep), and optionally add a note—all without creating an account. Manual offline .daylio backups are free, while automatic iCloud/Google Drive backup, unlimited custom moods, PDF/CSV export, and PIN lock require Premium.

Who it suits: People who want to link daily habits and activities to their mood, want personal iCloud/Google Drive backups, and are comfortable paying for Premium if they outgrow the 5-mood free limit.

Trade-off: Key features like unlimited moods, automatic backups, and deeper statistics sit behind a paid subscription, and auto-backups do not sync directly between Android and iOS.

App Store (iOS) Google Play (Android) Official Site
03 Nonprofit Emotion Science
100% Free (Nonprofit)

How We Feel

Created by a nonprofit with researchers at the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence, How We Feel organizes emotions into four energy/pleasantness quadrants, pairs check-ins with short science-backed regulation videos, and keeps personal entries local on your device without requiring an account.

Who it suits: People who want guided emotional-regulation tools, Apple Watch check-ins, or optional real-time sharing with trusted friends—all completely free.

Trade-off: Uses a fixed research-backed emotion taxonomy rather than letting you invent unlimited custom moods/icons, and its interface is geared toward reflective exercises rather than quick icon-grid logging.

App Store (iOS) Google Play (Android) Official Site
04 Symptom & Factor Correlations
Freemium

Bearable

Bearable goes beyond mood logging to track symptoms, sleep, medications, nutrition, energy, and daily habits in one timeline. Daily logging and 30-day charts are free (plus CSV export), while 60/90/365-day insights and deeper factor-impact correlation reports require a paid subscription (with sponsorship available via Bearable Heroes).

Who it suits: People managing chronic health conditions, medication changes, or sleep patterns who want cloud-synced multi-variable correlation graphs to review with a clinician.

Trade-off: Requires an account and stores entries in the cloud (no local-only mode), includes standard app analytics SDKs, and can feel heavier if you only want a quick mood check-in.
