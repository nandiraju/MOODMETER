Quick Support Answers

How do I cancel a subscription?

There is nothing to cancel—Mood Meter has zero subscriptions, zero free-trial traps, and zero in-app charges.

Can you reset my account or recover entries?

Mood Meter has no accounts or cloud servers. All entries live locally on your phone, so only you have custody of them.

Reporting a bug?

Mention whether you're on iOS or Android and what screen you were viewing—screenshots are always helpful.
