Local-First Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

At Mood Meter, your emotional reflections belong exclusively to you—stored 100% locally on your phone with zero cloud servers, zero accounts, and zero trackers.

Effective Date: February 14, 2026 • Last Updated: November 17, 2026

100% On-Device

Mood logs, intensity ratings, custom vibes, and journal notes never leave your phone or tablet.

Zero SDK Trackers

No ad networks, no Firebase/Google Analytics, no Meta SDK, and zero third-party data brokers.

No Sign-Up Ever

We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or social login credentials.

01 Core Principles

Privacy by Architecture

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the Mood Meter mobile app on iOS and Android (the "App") and visit moodmeter.cend.in.

  • Self-Custody: Your reflections stay inside your device's sandboxed storage.
  • Zero Monetization: We never sell, rent, or monetize personal or emotional data.
  • Offline-Ready: Every feature works in Airplane Mode with zero network calls.
02 Local Data Scope

What Lives on Your Device

The App stores only what you explicitly create inside your device's local application storage:

Mood Entries & Notes: Selected mood icons, 1–5 intensity scores, timestamps, and optional reflections.
Custom Vibes & Theme: Personal emotion labels/icons you create and your Light/Dark mode choice.
03 Zero Telemetry

What We Never Collect

Unlike cloud-backed apps, Mood Meter does not collect or transmit automated technical telemetry to external servers:

  • No IP addresses, GPS, or location tracking
  • No advertising IDs (IDFA / GAID) or device fingerprinting
  • No behavioral analytics, heatmaps, or third-party crash trackers
04 On-Device Processing

How Local Data Is Used

All computations happen locally on your device processor solely to power your experience:

  • Render your searchable chronological journal in the History tab.
  • Calculate your Current Vibe Streak, Vibe Power Gauge, 7-day Intensity Trend, and Hourly Activity charts.
  • Preserve your custom mood vocabulary and display preferences.
05 Security, Retention & Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Complete Control in Your Hands

Because your data never touches a central server, its security relies on your operating system's app sandboxing and your device lock (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or passcode). We cannot view, access, or recover your entries if your device is lost or reset. You can exercise full data rights directly inside the App at any moment:

Instant Access

Browse and search 100% of your logged moods and notes anytime in History.

Easy Correction

Tap any past entry or Custom Vibe to update its rating, note, timestamp, or label.

Granular Deletion

Remove individual journal entries or custom moods with one tap inside the App.

Complete Erasure

Uninstalling Mood Meter permanently wipes all local app storage from your device.

06 Additional Disclosures

Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Children's Privacy: Mood Meter does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Downloading the App via the Apple App Store or Google Play is governed by Apple's or Google's respective store policies.

Updates to This Policy: If future optional features ever change how data is handled, we will update this page and revise the "Last Updated" date prior to release.
