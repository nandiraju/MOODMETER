Local-First Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 14, 2026 • Last Updated: November 17, 2026

At Mood Meter ("MoodMeter", "we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is our foundational design principle. We believe your emotional entries and personal reflections belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mood Meter mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App") and visit moodmeter.cend.in.

1. Core Privacy Principles

  • 100% On-Device Storage: Your mood entries, intensity ratings, custom vibes, and journal reflections are stored locally on your device—never on external servers operated by us.
  • No Account Required: Mood Meter never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or social sign-in.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal or emotional data with advertisers or data brokers.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Information You Create in the App

When you use Mood Meter, the App saves the following data solely inside your device's local application storage:

  • Selected mood icons, intensity ratings (1–5 scale), timestamps, and optional journal reflections.
  • Custom Vibes (custom mood labels and selected icons) created by you.
  • Appearance preferences (such as Dark Mode or Light Mode).

Automatically Collected Information

Mood Meter does not collect or transmit telemetry, crash analytics, IP addresses, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), location data, or behavioral tracking data to external servers.

3. How Local Data Is Used

All data stored on your device is processed locally on your phone or tablet solely to:

  • Display your chronological mood history and search results.
  • Compute on-device emotional analytics, including your Current Vibe Streak, Vibe Power Gauge, 7-day Intensity Trend, Vibe Distribution, and Hourly Activity charts.
  • Remember your custom vibes and display theme preference.

4. Data Security & Retention

Because your entries never leave your device, your data is protected by your operating system's sandboxing and your device's own security controls (such as Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint unlock, or device passcode).

We have no technical means to view, access, or recover your entries. You retain complete control over retention: you may edit or delete individual mood entries or custom vibes at any time inside the App, or permanently erase all local data by uninstalling the App.

5. No Third-Party Tracking SDKs

Mood Meter integrates zero third-party analytics, advertising, or profiling SDKs (such as Google Analytics/Firebase, Meta SDK, or ad networks). Downloading the App via the Apple App Store or Google Play Store is subject to Apple's or Google's respective platform privacy policies.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Standards)

Because all data resides locally on your device under your direct custody, you can exercise your rights immediately at any time without submitting a request:

  • Right to Access: View and search your complete history directly in the History and Analytics tabs.
  • Right to Rectification: Tap any logged mood or custom vibe to modify its intensity, reflection note, date/time, or label.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete individual entries inside the App or uninstall the App to wipe all local records immediately.

7. Children's Privacy

Mood Meter does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we introduce optional features in the future that affect how data is handled, we will update this page and revise the "Last Updated" date above prior to release.
