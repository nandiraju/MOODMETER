Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MoodMeter ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the MoodMeter mobile application (the "App").

1. About MoodMeter

MoodMeter is a personal mood-tracking application that allows users to record their mood entries, journal thoughts, and view insights through dashboards and analytics.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Mood entries and journal notes
  • Custom mood labels or tags
  • Optional profile information (if provided)

Automatically Collected Information

  • App usage analytics (feature usage, interaction events)
  • Device information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide mood tracking and dashboard insights
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and monitor app stability
  • Develop new features and analytics improvements

4. Data Storage

Your mood data may be stored locally on your device and/or securely in cloud storage depending on the features you choose to use. We take reasonable steps to protect your data using industry-standard security practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your mood entries or journal content with third parties except:

  • When required by law
  • To provide essential technical services (such as hosting or analytics)
  • With your explicit consent

6. Analytics and Third-Party Services

MoodMeter may use third-party analytics or crash reporting tools to understand app usage and improve performance. These services may collect anonymized technical information.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide app functionality or comply with legal obligations. You may delete your data at any time through the app (if supported) or by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request correction of data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

9. Children's Privacy

MoodMeter is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. However, no system is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
